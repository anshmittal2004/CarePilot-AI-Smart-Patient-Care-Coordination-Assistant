# Patient Onboarding
Patients can use the approved onboarding flow to verify identity, review consent, and access care-navigation services. If the required information is not available in the approved knowledge base, the assistant must not invent a policy.

# Appointments
Appointment scheduling, rescheduling, reminders, telehealth preparation, and visit preparation should follow the approved healthcare coordination workflow.

# Safety
The assistant provides informational navigation only. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or make clinical treatment decisions. Emergency or clinically sensitive questions should be escalated to an appropriate licensed healthcare professional.
